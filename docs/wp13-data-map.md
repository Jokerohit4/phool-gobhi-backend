# WP13 subscription funnel data map

`GuidanceProfile.userId` identifies the customer across the health, wallet and auth services. A reference by user ID is a service-level lookup. `sourceReportId` identifies the report confirmed for clinical guidance. Other uploads are listed by user ID. Paths below refer to the gateway unless labeled internal.

| Question or collected data | Stored in | Who can read it | Export and erasure | Profile location or reference |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Does the customer have diabetes or prediabetes? | `health.GuidanceProfile.hasCondition` | Customer via `/api/health/guidance/profile/condition` or `/api/health/guidance/me`. `gobhi` staff via guidance admin. Trusted services via the key-protected internal guidance context. The customer's wallet catalog exposes the same boolean and suggested family. | Full health export at `guidance.profile.hasCondition`. Account erasure deletes the `GuidanceProfile` row. | On the guidance profile. `null` means unanswered. |
| Which condition? | `health.GuidanceProfile.conditionType` | Customer, `gobhi` guidance staff and trusted internal guidance callers. | Full health export at `guidance.profile.conditionType`. Account erasure deletes the row. | On the guidance profile. A report-set answer starts with a null type. A legacy `statedCondition` can supply the type through checkout for a self-declared answer. |
| How and when was the answer recorded? | `health.GuidanceProfile.conditionSource`, `health.GuidanceProfile.conditionDeclaredAt` | Customer, `gobhi` guidance staff and trusted internal guidance callers. | Both fields are in `guidance.profile` and erased with the row. | On the guidance profile. Staff correcting an answer to true preserve the existing source. Staff correcting it to false clear the source. |
| City or region chosen for the offer | `health.GuidanceProfile.region`. `wallet.CoachingOrder.offerSnapshot.region` and `.city`, copied into a membership snapshot for the active term. | Customer, `gobhi` guidance staff and trusted internal guidance callers can read the normalized region. The owner can see the active order's city and region in coaching membership. Wallet fulfillment staff can see membership context. | Health export includes `guidance.profile.region`. Wallet export includes order and membership snapshots. Account erasure deletes the health profile. Wallet keeps the financial snapshots. | On the guidance profile as `GURUGRAM` or `REST_INDIA` after a v2 quote or order attempt with a valid offer and checkout term. Historical orders retain the submitted city. Browsing the catalog leaves the saved region as it was. |
| Lab report, permission to read it, date and reviewed values | `health.HealthReport.storagePath`, `fileName`, `mimeType`, `readConsentAt`, `reportDate`, `detectedReportDate`, `proposedProfile`, `proposedMarkers`, `proposedReadings`, `confirmedReadings`. File in private medical object storage. Extracted values in `health.ReportExtraction` by `reportId`. Confirmed name, age, gender, address and markers in `health.GuidanceProfile.confirmedDetails`, `confirmedMarkers` and `liverFlag`. Accepted biometric values also enter `health.BiometricEntry`. | Owner through `/api/health/guidance/me` and `/api/health/guidance/reports/:id` with a signed link from `/link`. `gobhi` staff have a report library and staff-only links through guidance admin. Report processing receives a signed link. | Full health export includes `guidance.reportReadings` (report ID, entered date and proposed and confirmed readings), confirmed guidance details and biometric history. The report file is available through its signed-link endpoint. Individual report deletion removes the report, cascades extracted rows, detaches clinical guidance, and attempts to delete the blob. It preserves the condition and region and leaves verified history entries. Account erasure also deletes biometric and profile rows and sweeps the user's medical object prefix. | The confirmed report is referenced by `GuidanceProfile.sourceReportId`. All uploaded reports are reachable by the profile's user ID in `/guidance/me`. |
| Model-training permission | `health.ModelTrainingConsent.grantedAt`, `revokedAt`, `policyVersion`, keyed by `userId` | Customer through `/api/health/model-training/consent`. Trusted callers through the key-protected internal consent route. | The customer can read and revoke consent. Account erasure deletes the consent row. The full health export on this branch omits this existing consent record. | Related by user ID. It is separate from report-reading permission. |
| Outcome of the first-log prompt | Client analytics `events_raw.event`, `events_raw.distinct_id`, and `action` in the JSON stored in `events_raw.properties` for `onboarding_log_prompt_shown` and `onboarding_log_prompt_completed`. `action` is `report`, `meal`, or `workout` on completion. | Authorized analytics readers. The backend has no customer-facing prompt-history read. | The prompt events are outside current account export and erasure. Warehouse reporting filters erased actors where that suppression is configured. The actual report, meal or workout has its own health export and erasure paths. | Analytics only. There is no profile reference or durable backend answer for dismissing the prompt. |
| Session confirmation, dispute and resolution | `wallet.CoachingSessionLog.status`, `confirmedAt`, `disputeReason`, `resolution`, `resolvedAt`, `resolvedBy`. Session context in `kind`, `mode`, `heldAt`, `durationMinutes`, `note`, `loggedBy`, `membershipOrderId`. | Customer can read their own session view at `/api/wallet/coaching/sessions/mine`. `gobhi` operations can read and resolve disputed sessions. | Wallet erasure deletes the user's session logs. The wallet account export on this branch omits the existing session log. | Related by user ID. The session also references the membership's purchase order. |
| Coupon code and redemption | `wallet.Coupon.code`, `wallet.CouponRedemption.couponId`, `orderId`, `userId`, `status`. Applied code also in `wallet.CoachingOrder.offerSnapshot.price.couponCode` (v2) or `.discount.couponCode` (legacy). | Customer gets their quote and own order data. `gobhi` coupon admins and wallet checkout can read coupon configuration and redemption use. | Wallet export includes the applied code in the order snapshot. Redemption and order rows follow wallet financial retention. Rejected attempts have no customer coupon row and can appear in registered analytics. | Related by user ID and order ID. No coupon field on the guidance profile. |
| Offer purchased, renewal and payment | `wallet.CoachingOrder.product`, `offerSnapshot`, `amountPaise`, `isRenewal`, `termStartsAt`, `status`, `orderId`, `paymentId`, `refundId` and timestamps. `wallet.CoachingMembership.activeOrderId`, `scheduledOrderId`, `offerSnapshot` and term/status fields. | Customer through coaching membership, checkout/verification and their export. `gobhi` fulfillment operations and trusted wallet callers can read membership. Razorpay receives checkout and payment data. | Wallet export includes both rows. Wallet erasure deactivates membership and removes operational fulfillment details, retaining the financial orders. | Related by user ID. Membership points to active and scheduled orders. |
| Add-on chosen, quantity and balance | `wallet.CoachingOrder.isAddon`, `product`, `offerSnapshot.kind`, `offerSnapshot.price.quantity`. Remaining v2 trainer or nutritionist sessions in `wallet.CoachingMembership.trainerSessionsRemaining` and `nutritionistSessionsRemaining`. Legacy grants in `wallet.CoachingAddonGrant`, lab requests in `wallet.CoachingFulfillment`, and gym balances managed by booking service. | Customer through coaching membership and checkout. `gobhi` fulfillment staff and trusted entitlement callers see the relevant operational data. | Wallet export includes orders, membership, legacy grants and fulfillment. Wallet erasure zeroes wallet balances and grants, clears fulfillment details, and retains the purchase. | Related by user ID and the membership's purchase order. |
| Date of birth for adult eligibility and existing gym selection | `auth.User.dateOfBirth`, `linkedGymId`, `homeUnclaimedGymId`, `currentlyWorksOut`, `trainingLocationPref`. Wallet also maintains `wallet.CoachingMembership.hasExistingGym`. | Customer via their account and coaching membership. Trusted auth callers use those fields for eligibility and fulfillment. | Auth profile export and account deletion cover the account profile. Wallet exports its membership and clears the existing-gym flag on erasure. | Date of birth and the gym choices are on the account profile. Wallet membership is looked up by user ID. |

The health export reads the full guidance profile row. The health erasure routine deletes the row by user ID. All five WP13 fields follow those paths. The main export query puts `foodRequest` immediately after `healthProfileConsent`, so medical documents, the profile, and the consent fields feed their matching export slots.
